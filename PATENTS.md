# Patent commitment for implementers

The Apache License 2.0 ([LICENSE](LICENSE)) gives a patent license to people who use the code in this repository. Someone who implements the standards from their texts alone, in their own code, needs a commitment of their own. This is it.

## The commitment

Deskree Technologies Inc. makes the **Open Web Foundation Final Specification Agreement, OWFa 1.0** ([text](https://www.openwebfoundation.org/the-agreements/the-owf-1-0-agreements-granted-claims/owfa-1-0)), for each specification version listed below. Under it, Deskree gives anyone who implements that version a royalty-free, worldwide copyright license to the text and a promise not to assert its patent claims that the version necessarily requires, under the agreement's terms (including its defensive termination).

| Specification | Version | What it includes | Signed agreement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core | `obi/0.1` at its baseline ([CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md)) | [spec/protocol.md](spec/protocol.md), [spec/identity.md](spec/identity.md), [spec/sphere-api.md](spec/sphere-api.md), [spec/schemas/v0.1/](spec/schemas/v0.1/) and [spec/conformance/](spec/conformance/) | *to be linked once signed* |

Each later version of a standard is added to this table when it is published, with its own signed agreement. Once a version is listed, the commitment for it is not withdrawn.

## What this doesn't cover

- Patents of anyone other than Deskree.
- Anything beyond what a listed version necessarily requires, as the agreement defines it.
- Names and marks: see [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).

Contributions to the standards come in under the Apache License 2.0, signed off under the DCO ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)), which carries each contributor's own patent license for what they contribute.
