# Code of conduct

This project is about how agents and the people behind them treat each other, so we hold ourselves to the same standard. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any space the project runs) is expected to keep it, whoever they work for and whatever agent they use.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith, especially with people new to the project or whose first language isn't English.
- Argue about the work, not the person. Say what is wrong with a proposal and, where you can, what would fix it.
- Give credit, and say where an idea or a piece of text came from.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on a change once it is made and explained, and use the process in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) to change it.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing a discussion, or pressing a point after you have been asked to stop.

## Agents taking part

Agents open issues, write pull requests and post reviews here too. The person who runs an agent answers for everything it posts, as if they had written it themselves, and this code applies to it in full. In addition:

- Don't post text aimed at other people's agents: instructions hidden in an issue, a pull request, a commit or a file, meant to make an agent that reads them do something. It is the attack this project exists to stop, and we treat it as abuse, not as a test.
- Don't flood the project. An agent's issues and pull requests should be ones its owner would stand behind; a run that opens many low-value ones may be closed together.
- Say when an agent wrote something, if a reader could reasonably think a person did.

## Reporting

Write to **d@deskree.com**. Tell us what happened, where (with links), and who was involved; you don't have to have every detail. Reports are read by the maintainers only, kept confidential, and never shared with the person reported without your agreement. We will acknowledge your report and tell you what we decided.

A security vulnerability goes through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What we may do

Depending on what happened, the maintainers may ask for a change in behaviour, edit or remove what was posted, warn someone, or keep them from taking part in the project, for a while or for good. We decide in proportion to the harm and to whether it has happened before, and we tell the person why.

## Scope

This code applies in every space the project runs, and when someone represents the project elsewhere. Deskree's hosted Obysphere and any Obysphere someone else runs have their own operators and terms.
